One swipe per email

Swipe right to reply

Write it yourself or draft one with your own AI key. Attach photos and files, and your signature goes on automatically.

Swipe left to archive

Done with it. It leaves your inbox on the server too, so every device stays in sync.

Swipe up to star or forward

Left half stars it for later. Right half forwards it, attachments included.

Swipe down to delete or mark spam

Left half deletes. Right half sends it to spam.

Hold to mark as read

Hold a card for a second and it's marked read and out of your stack for good.

Undo anything

Swiped the wrong way? Bring the last card straight back.

The important email comes first

Every card gets a color, from green (must answer) through amber (worth a look) to red (probably noise). Thumbmail works it out on your phone from how often someone emails you, how often and how quickly you reply, and how long your replies are. Mailing lists and no-reply senders wait at the back.

With Pro you can pin people or whole domains to the top, push others to the bottom, lift or sink cards by keyword, and give each inbox its own rules.

Built to stay out of your way

No Thumbmail server

Your mail goes straight between your iPhone and your email provider. We never see it.

Face ID lock

Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode.

Reminders that help

A nudge at 8 AM and 5 PM when your stack has mail, and alerts for important new email only.
